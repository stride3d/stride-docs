You don't run these steps yourself: Game Studio runs them when you open a project made with an older version, and `stride upgrade` runs them from the command line. Each section says what its step changes, and how to make the change by hand when the step fails: the upgrade report (`upgrade-report.md`) links each step to its section. See [the upgrade guide](../upgrade/index.md) for how steps run and what to do when one fails.
