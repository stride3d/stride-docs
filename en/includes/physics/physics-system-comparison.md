[Bullet](../../manual/physics-bullet/index.md) is the older physics system that's currently the default. It's **not as performant** as Bepu, but it's **mature** and **relaiable.**

[Bepu](../../manual/physics/index.md) is the new modern replacement for Bullet. It's **faster**, supports **multithreading** and it tends to receive **more updates** than its predecessor. However, it **lacks certain features of Bullet,** most notably [navigation](../../manual/navigation/index.md).

> [!TIP]
> We recommend switching to [Bepu](../../manual/physics/index.md), unless your project relies on navigation.
