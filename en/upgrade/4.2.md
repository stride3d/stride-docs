# Upgrade to Stride 4.2

## Upgrade steps

The steps that upgrade a project from a version older than 4.2.

[!INCLUDE [upgrade-steps-intro](../includes/upgrade-steps-intro.md)]

### Stride-4.2.0-P1 Target .NET 8 instead of .NET 6

Stride 4.2 runs on .NET 8. The step changes `net6.0` to `net8.0` in the `TargetFramework` of the project, platform suffix included (`net6.0-windows` becomes `net8.0-windows`).

**By hand:** in the `.csproj`, change `net6.0` to `net8.0` in `<TargetFramework>`. The step of 4.3 then moves it on to .NET 10.
