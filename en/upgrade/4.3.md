# Upgrade to Stride 4.3

## Upgrade steps

The steps that upgrade a project from a version older than 4.3.

[!INCLUDE [upgrade-steps-intro](../includes/upgrade-steps-intro.md)]

### Stride-4.3.0-P1 Target .NET 10 instead of .NET 8

Stride 4.3 runs on .NET 10. The step changes `net8.0` to `net10.0` in the `TargetFramework` of the project, platform suffix included (`net8.0-windows` becomes `net10.0-windows`).

**By hand:** in the `.csproj`, change `net8.0` to `net10.0` in `<TargetFramework>`, and install the .NET 10 SDK if you don't have it.
