# Solution explorer

<span class="badge text-bg-primary">Beginner</span>

The **Solution explorer** panel shows the structure of your project. It features a list of [project packages](../../files-and-folders/project-packages/index.md) and their contents.

Selecting folders in the **Solution explorer** also selects them in the **Asset view**.
