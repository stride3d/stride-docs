# Scenes

**Scenes** are loadable containers that hold a collection of entities. They can be used for creating levels or splitting off sections of your game into dynamically loaded sections for optimized performance.

## Sub-scenes

On top of containing multiple entities and their children, scenes can also hold other scenes in themselves.

An example of this would be a scene called **Level1** containing sub-scenes with different sections that are dynamically loaded depending on where the player is in the level.

For more information on sub-scenes, visit the [sub-scenes page](sub-scenes.md)
