# Scenes

<span class="badge text-bg-primary">Beginner</span>
<span class="badge text-bg-success">Level designer</span>
<span class="badge text-bg-success">Programmer</span>

In Stride, **scenes** make up the environment of your game. They are **loadable containers** that hold entities and can be used for creating **levels** or splitting the world into **dynamically loaded sections** for optimized performance.
