# Scenes

In Stride, **scenes** make up the environment of your game. They are **loadable containers** that hold [entities](entities/index.md) and can be used for creating **levels** or splitting the world into **dynamically loaded sections** for optimized performance.

Scenes are populated by [entities](entities/index.md) that along with their [components](entities/components.md) define how the world looks and behaves.
